About PU-Marketplace

We're reimagining campus commerce — making it safe, simple, and social for every student.

🎯 Our Mission

PU-Marketplace connects university students in a trusted community to buy, sell, and trade everything they need for campus life. From textbooks to electronics, from furniture to services — we make campus commerce frictionless and safe.

We believe that students deserve a marketplace built by students, for students. One where verification happens automatically, where meetings are on campus, and where every transaction is protected.

💡 Why We Started

Our founder, after frustrating experiences selling textbooks on sketchy websites and buying furniture from strangers, realized there had to be a better way. A platform exclusively for campus students — where you know everyone is real, nearby, and trustworthy.

Today, thousands of students use PU-Marketplace every day to make smarter trades and build their campus community.
