Privacy Policy

Last updated: January 2026

1. Introduction

PU-Marketplace ("we" or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our Platform.

2. Information We Collect

Account Information: When you sign up, we collect your name, email, university, phone number (optional), and profile information.

Transaction Data: We collect information about your listings and purchases, including descriptions, prices, and payment methods.

Communication Data: Messages you send through our in-app chat system.

Device Information: IP address, browser type, operating system, and device identifiers.

Usage Data: How you interact with the Platform, including pages viewed, clicks, and search queries.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:

  • Verify your student status and age
  • Process transactions and prevent fraud
  • Improve our service and user experience
  • Send important notifications and updates
  • Respond to your inquiries and support requests
  • Comply with legal obligations

4. Data Sharing

We do NOT sell your data. We may share information with:

  • Other users (limited to what's necessary for transactions)
  • Payment processors and banks
  • Law enforcement (if legally required)
  • Service providers under strict confidentiality agreements

5. Security

We use industry-standard encryption and security measures to protect your data. However, no system is 100% secure. We recommend using strong passwords and not sharing your account credentials.

6. Your Rights

You have the right to access, update, or delete your personal information at any time. Contact us at privacy@pu-marketplace.com to exercise these rights.

7. Cookies

We use cookies to improve your experience. You can disable cookies in your browser settings, but some features may not work properly.

8. Contact Us

If you have privacy concerns, contact us at privacy@pu-marketplace.com
