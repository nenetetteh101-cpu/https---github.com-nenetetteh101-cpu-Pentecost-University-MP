How We Started

A Dorm Room Idea That Grew Into a Movement

It started in 2024 when two final-year engineering students — Kwame and Abena — tried to sell their old textbooks before graduating. They posted on WhatsApp groups, got buried in noise, and eventually gave the books away for free out of frustration.

That weekend, they sketched out what would become PU-Marketplace: a dedicated, trusted space for campus commerce. No strangers, no spam — just students helping students.

Within three months of launching, over 800 students had signed up. Within a year, PU-Marketplace had processed more than GH₵ 500,000 in campus trades. Today, it's the go-to platform for everything from textbooks to tech, services to furniture — all within the university gates.

Our Journey

From Idea to Institution

Jan '24
The Idea

Two frustrated final-year students sketch the concept of a campus-only marketplace after a failed attempt to sell textbooks via WhatsApp groups.

Mar '24
First Launch

PU-Marketplace launches in beta to 50 hand-picked students. First transaction: a used mechanical engineering textbook sold for GH₵ 40.

Jun '24
800 Students & Counting

Word-of-mouth drives organic growth. The platform hits 800 registered users and processes its first GH₵ 100,000 in transactions.

Oct '24
Services & Escrow

We launch the Services category and a secure escrow payment system, expanding beyond physical goods to tutoring, design, and campus delivery.

Jan '25
University Recognition

The university officially endorses PU-Marketplace as a supported student initiative and integrates it into the student onboarding pack.

Mar '26
Today — 5,000+ Strong

5,000+ registered students, GH₵ 2M+ in value traded, and a full team of student developers and operators. The mission continues.
