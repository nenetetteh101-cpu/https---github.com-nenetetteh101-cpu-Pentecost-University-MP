📋 How a Trade Works
1
Find a listing you like
Browse Products or Services. Save to Wishlist and tap any listing for full details and the seller's rating.
2
Message the seller in-app
Tap "Message Seller" to open secure in-app chat. Ask questions, negotiate price — all within PU-Marketplace.
3
Agree on a campus meetup spot
Choose a busy, well-lit location — Library Foyer, Student Union, or your faculty common area. Bring a friend for high-value trades.
4
Inspect, agree, and exchange
Check the item thoroughly. Payment is handled directly between you and the seller — cash or whatever you both agree on.
5
Mark complete & review
Confirm the trade in the app and leave an honest review to build the seller's (and your) campus reputation.

❓ Common Questions
Why doesn't PU-Marketplace handle payments?▼
Keeping payments between buyers and sellers directly keeps things simple, transparent, and free. You're always in full control of your money. We provide the tools to connect — you decide how to transact.
What if a seller doesn't show up?▼
Report it through the app — tap the seller's profile and select "Report". Our team reviews within 2 hours and may issue a warning or suspension. This is why all communication should stay in-app: there's always a record.
Can I negotiate the price in chat?▼
Yes. The in-app chat is the right place to discuss price, condition, and meetup details. Listings marked "Fixed Price" indicate the seller prefers not to negotiate.
What if the item isn't as described?▼
You are never obligated to complete a trade. If an item is significantly different from its listing, walk away and report it. Leaving an honest review also helps other students stay informed.
Is the in-app chat safe?▼
Yes — all participants are verified campus users. Your phone number and email are never shared. If someone makes you uncomfortable, report them immediately via the chat's report button.
